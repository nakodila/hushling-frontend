How it works

Three small steps, one quiet ritual

No studio, no singing lessons — just your phone and a few quiet minutes.

Record your voice

Read a short passage aloud — about a minute. That's all Lallababy needs to learn how you sound.

Shape the lullaby

Pick a melody, a mood — dreamy, playful, calm — and add names or details that make it theirs.

Receive your lullaby

In minutes, a full lullaby arrives — sung in your voice — ready to play tonight, and every night after.

Your voice, your rules

Recordings stay private, always

A voice is personal — especially a parent's. Lallababy only ever uses your recording to make lullabies for you, never to train shared models, and never shared with anyone else.

  • Delete your voice model any time
  • Lullabies stay in your account only
  • No recording is ever used to train other users' voices
